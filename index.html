Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. The free plan starts the moment you register, with no card and no trial clock. You can build, publish and add up to 3 offers for as long as you like.

What happens when I pass 3 offers?

The builder asks you to upgrade. Pro allows 500 offers, Agency 2000. Your existing site and offers stay exactly as they are.

Can customers open my site without an account?

Yes. Your site has a public link that anyone can open on a phone or a computer.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, with Agency. Free and Pro use a GoldOffer-hosted link.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

1. Who we are

GoldOffer Studio is a service for building websites for small businesses. The data controller is Rayan Katsarov. Questions: rayankatsarov2013@gmail.com, phone 0896 255 898.

2. What data we collect

  • Account: first name, last name, email and password (the password is stored only in hashed form).
  • Site content: business name, city, offers, prices, photos, and the contact details you choose to enter (phone, address, opening hours, social links).
  • Requests and messages from your customers: the name and contact details the customer types in the form, the chosen offer, any notes, the messages exchanged in the chat, and the offers a visitor saves as favorites (those are kept only in the visitor's own browser).
  • In your browser: we store only your login token and your language choice. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track visitors.

3. Why we use the data

  • To provide the service: your account, publishing your site, showing your offers.
  • To email you when a new request arrives and to send a password reset link.
  • To take payment for the subscription, which is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • For security and to prevent abuse.

4. Legal basis

Performance of a contract (providing the service), your consent (the request form and registration) and legitimate interest (security).

5. Who else receives data

We use providers that process data on our behalf: hosting (Render), database (MongoDB Atlas), email sending (Brevo or Resend) and payments (Stripe). Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the builder shows photos from Wikipedia. If a site shows a map, it loads from Google Maps. We do not sell data.

6. Your customers' data

When a customer sends a request through your site, you are the controller of that data and GoldOffer Studio only processes it to pass it to you. You are responsible for using it lawfully.

7. How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account or ask us to, we delete the data unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing. Write to rayankatsarov2013@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg).

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
